# I Survived 30 Days in a Zombie City (script, ~15 min)

Voices: **ELI** (hero, narrator, Tyler) · **ANCHOR** (news, female) · **MOM** (phone, older female) · **DALE** (old neighbor, gravelly male) · **RILEY** (teen girl) · **RADIO** (deep male, military)
`[SFX: ...]` = sound effect. Background changes every 5–7 seconds (each paragraph = 1–2 shots). No music.

## HOOK (0:00)

ELI: On day one, a man bit a stranger on my street. Everybody filmed it.
ELI: By day ten, there was nobody left to film.
[SFX: thunder crack]
ELI: I survived thirty days in a dead city, with a colander on my head, a neighbor who never stopped talking, and one rule. Never open the door at night.
ELI: On day twenty-nine, I broke that rule. Here is every single day.

## DAY 1

ELI: Day one. A normal Tuesday. Except people were acting strange. The man at the bus stop was staring at the sun. A woman walked straight into traffic and did not even blink.
[SFX: car horn, tires screech]
ELI: Then a guy in a suit grabbed a stranger, and bit him. Right on the neck. Phones came out. Nobody helped.
[SFX: scream, crowd panic]
ELI: I did the smartest thing I have ever done. I ran to my car.
[SFX: car door, engine start]

## DAY 2

[SFX: phone ringing]
MOM: Eli? Honey, are you watching the news? Do not go outside.
ELI: Mom, I'm fine. It is probably nothing.
[SFX: TV static]
ANCHOR: Authorities are calling it an aggressive flu. Please stay calm, and stay indoors.
ELI: The anchor was sweating. Behind her, somebody was screaming. Then the channel went black.

## DAY 3

ELI: Day three. The power was still on, so I filled every pot, every bottle, and the bathtub with water. Then I took the colander from the kitchen and put it on my head. It is not a helmet. But it felt like one.
[SFX: knocking on door]
DALE: Kid! It's Dale, from 4B. You got any batteries?
ELI: That was Mister Dale. Seventy years old, and louder than the sirens.

## DAY 4

ELI: Day four. The sirens stopped. That was worse. From my window I counted forty of them on the street, just standing there. Waiting.
[SFX: wind, distant moan]
ELI: One of them looked up. Straight at my window. I did not sleep that night.

## DAY 5

[SFX: thunder, heavy rain]
ELI: Day five. A storm hit the city. Lightning showed me something I did not want to see. They do not hide from rain. They do not feel it at all.
ELI: But thunder makes them turn. Every single one. So they follow sound. I wrote that on my wall.

## DAY 6

ELI: Day six. The food in my fridge was going bad, so Dale and I made a deal. He has a gas stove. I have the food. We cooked everything in one night.
DALE: Best steak of my life. Shame about the end of the world.

## DAY 7

[SFX: electric buzz, power down]
ELI: Day seven. The power died. The whole city went dark at once. And in the dark, I finally heard how many there were.
[SFX: hundreds of moans]

## DAY 8

ELI: Day eight. We needed supplies. The pharmacy was two blocks away. Two blocks never felt so long.
[SFX: glass crunch underfoot]
ELI: I stepped on broken glass. One of them heard it. Then five of them heard it.
[SFX: running footsteps, door slam]
ELI: I got back with bandages, painkillers, and a new respect for quiet shoes.

## DAY 9

[SFX: phone ringing]
ELI: Day nine. My phone rang. One bar of signal.
MOM: Eli, we are at the stadium. The army is here. They say it is safe.
[SFX: call drops, beep]
ELI: The line died. That was the last time my phone ever rang.

## DAY 10

ELI: Day ten. I walked to the roof and looked at the city. No cars. No lights. No people. Just smoke, and them.
ELI: That is when I understood. Nobody was coming.

## DAY 11

ELI: Day eleven. Dale found an old radio in the basement.
[SFX: radio static]
RADIO: This is the military safe zone at the harbor. We have food, water, and walls. Evacuation boats leave on day thirty. After that, we are gone.
DALE: The harbor? Kid, that's across the whole city.
ELI: Nineteen days. One city. I started drawing a map.

## DAY 12

ELI: Day twelve. We practiced being quiet. Dale failed.
[SFX: metal pots crashing]
DALE: Sorry!
ELI: Three of them walked into our hallway. We stood behind the door for six hours.

## DAY 13

ELI: Day thirteen. I tested my storm idea. I threw an alarm clock off the roof.
[SFX: alarm clock ringing, fading away]
ELI: The whole street followed it. Every one of them. For the first time, I smiled. I had a weapon. Noise.

## DAY 14

[SFX: crying, quiet]
ELI: Day fourteen. I heard crying in the stairwell. Rule one says, never open the door. But this was not a moan.
RILEY: Please. I'm not bitten. I swear.
ELI: Her name was Riley. Sixteen. She had been alone in apartment 2A for two weeks, eating cereal with water.

## DAY 15

ELI: Day fifteen. Halfway. Riley knew things we did not.
RILEY: They are slower in the morning. And they hate the cold.
ELI: She had been watching them from her window, and writing it all down. The kid was smarter than both of us.

## DAY 16

ELI: Day sixteen. Water was almost gone. So we put every bucket on the roof and waited.
[SFX: thunder, rain]
ELI: The storm came back. This time, I was happy to hear it.

## DAY 17

ELI: Day seventeen. Something changed. One of them stood at our building door, and pulled the handle.
[SFX: door handle rattling]
RILEY: They never did that before.
ELI: They were learning. And we were running out of time.

## DAY 18

ELI: Day eighteen. We blocked the stairs with every piece of furniture in the building. Dale gave up his favorite chair.
DALE: Forty years I had that chair.

## DAY 19

ELI: Day nineteen. Food run. The supermarket on the corner. It looked empty.
[SFX: fluorescent lights buzzing on]
ELI: Then every light in the store turned on at once. A generator. And in the light, I saw them. In every aisle.
[SFX: shopping cart crash]
ELI: I left with four cans of beans, and my life.

## DAY 20

ELI: Day twenty. Ten days left. The plan was simple. Find a car. Drive to the harbor. Do not die.
DALE: I like the last part.

## DAY 21

ELI: Day twenty-one. My car was still on the street. Under about thirty of them. We needed another one.
RILEY: The delivery van. Behind the bakery. Keys are in it. I saw it from my window.

## DAY 22

ELI: Day twenty-two. We got to the van at sunrise, when they are slow. It had half a tank, and it smelled like old bread.
[SFX: engine struggling, then starting]
ELI: It was the most beautiful sound I ever heard.

## DAY 23

[SFX: glass breaking, moans]
ELI: Day twenty-three. They broke through the front door of our building. The furniture held them for one hour.
ELI: We packed everything we had into three bags. Nobody talked.

## DAY 24

ELI: Day twenty-four. We left through the fire escape. I looked back at my window one last time. Twenty-four days. That little apartment kept me alive.
[SFX: van door slam, engine]

## DAY 25

ELI: Day twenty-five. The highway was a wall of dead cars. So we drove through the old town. Slow. Lights off.
[SFX: thunder, far away]
RILEY: Eli. Look at the bridge.
ELI: The bridge to the harbor was gone. Blown up. By the army.

## DAY 26

ELI: Day twenty-six. We slept in a school. On the chalkboard, somebody had written the names of forty children. And one sentence. We went to the harbor.
[SFX: school bell ringing]
ELI: Then the school bell rang. On its own. And they started coming.

## DAY 27

ELI: Day twenty-seven. Riley found another way on my map. The train tunnel. It goes under the river.
DALE: Dark tunnel full of who knows what. Wonderful.

## DAY 28

[SFX: dripping water, echo]
ELI: Day twenty-eight. The tunnel. One flashlight. Three people. We walked for four hours and heard them breathing in the dark the whole way.
ELI: We did not run. Running is loud.

## DAY 29

ELI: Day twenty-nine. We came out one mile from the harbor, and hid in a ticket booth for the night.
[SFX: knocking, slow]
ELI: At midnight, somebody knocked. A voice said, help me. I knew the rule. Never open the door at night.
ELI: I opened it.
[SFX: thunder crack, scream]
ELI: It was not a person. Dale pulled me back, and took the bite that was meant for me.
DALE: Go, kid. I'm too old for boats anyway. Take the girl. And take my radio.
[SFX: alarm clock ringing]
ELI: He walked into the street with my last alarm clock ringing in his hand. And every one of them followed him.

## DAY 30

[SFX: boat horn]
ELI: Day thirty. Riley and I reached the harbor at sunrise. The last boat was still there.
RADIO: Two survivors at the gate! Open it! Open it now!
[SFX: metal gate, waves]
ELI: Thirty days. I lost my home, my city, and the loudest friend I ever had.
ELI: I still wear the colander. And I still follow one rule. But now it is a different one. If somebody is crying, open the door.
[SFX: radio static]
RADIO: ...any survivors... the island is not safe... repeat, the island is not safe...
ELI: That is when day thirty-one started.
